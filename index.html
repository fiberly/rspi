01 — Profile

A Challengers career built in public.

Nicholas “Rspi” Barton is an American Call of Duty player. He competes in North America on controller and is currently a free agent. His last listed team is OMNiA x Stallions.

From February 2025 through June 2026 he played for Luxury Exotics, FiveFears White, FiveFears Black, the Huntsmen, Team Orchid, CABAL Esports, and OMNiA x Stallions. The longest of those runs was the Huntsmen, December 4, 2025 to April 7, 2026, alongside Kips, Nium, and Renegade, with Methodz as general manager and H3CZ as owner.

With CABAL Esports he finished first or second in the CDC North America 2026 Elite Stage 4 season and second in the Stage 4 Playoffs, an event listed with a $10,000 prize. On July 5, 2026 he placed second at the CDC North America Last Chance Qualifier with Arcitys, Peel, and Priestahh.

This page uses the Call of Duty Esports Wiki only. The wiki biography is empty, so nothing here is a personal story beyond the published team history, results, and press. Prize figures are the amounts printed on the wiki, not confirmed personal earnings.
